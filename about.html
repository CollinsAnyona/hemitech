Our Story

Built from a conviction, not a gap in the market.

HemiTech was founded in 2024 by Collins O. Junior — a software engineering graduate and data scientist who had seen firsthand how organizations struggled when their technology and brand identity didn't speak the same language.

The vision was simple: build a studio where engineers and designers work as one team, not two departments. Where a client's backend infrastructure and their visual identity are treated with equal seriousness and craft.

Since launching, we've delivered projects across web development, data systems, brand identity, and creative design — serving clients who expect both precision and impact, with a growing portfolio to show for it.
