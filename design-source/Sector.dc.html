What usually goes wrong

The failures are rarely technical.

A public service that works in the office and fails on a phone in Kitui has not been delivered. These are the four we are called in to fix.

It is too heavy for the connection it is used on

Portals designed on office fibre and opened on 3G. The citizen gives up, then queues at the counter instead — and the system is recorded as unused rather than unusable.

Nobody can answer a question about it

Applications are recorded but never counted. When the county assembly asks how many permits were issued last quarter, somebody spends three days in a spreadsheet.

Personal data is collected without a basis

Forms gathering ID numbers and phone numbers with no privacy notice, no retention rule and no record of consent. The Data Protection Act applies to public bodies too.

The vendor left and took the keys

No source code, no documentation, no access to the hosting account. A change that should take a day becomes a new procurement.
