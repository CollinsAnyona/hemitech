The locked brand, extended to a website.

Nothing here invents a new colour or a second typeface. It takes the creative system locked on 8 September and adds what a website needs and a social post does not: interaction states, form controls, a spacing scale, and contrast verified against WCAG rather than assumed.

The ground is never flat

White at the top easing to #F3F8FE at the bottom, plus two off-canvas corner glows — BLUE_LGT top-right at ~17%, BLUE_MID bottom-left at ~12%. The glows stay in the corners. A glow that reaches the centre of a page produces a grey blotch behind the text, which is exactly what went wrong on the first bright poster in August.

03 · Layout

Grid, spacing, and where the air goes.

Canvas

1440 desktop, 1200 content maximum, 60px gutters. 390 mobile with 22px gutters. Nothing is centred except closing cards.

Spacing scale

4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 22 · 32 · 44 · 56 · 76 · 96. Sections are 70–96px on desktop, 44–48px on mobile.

Rhythm

Siblings are laid out with flex or grid and gap, never per-element margins. If a layout feels tight, cut copy — do not shrink the air.

Radius & elevation

4px on controls, 5px on cards, 6px on full-width panels. One shadow, used only on a card that genuinely floats. Not everything is a card.

The rule

6px, fully rounded, 180px, BLUE_MID → BLUE_DEEP. Sits under the headline. Never a flat navy line.

05 · Accessibility and motion

We are about to sell this as a service.

Which means the site has to hold to it first. Public-sector buyers increasingly score accessibility in evaluation, and a supplier whose own site fails is an easy mark down.

Contrast

WCAG 2.2 AA throughout, verified by calculation. Two pairs were caught and changed during this design: small ordinals moved from BLUE_LGT (3.53) to BLUE_MID (5.71), and footer secondary text from #5B7AB5 (4.24) to #8FB4E8 (8.54).

Targets and focus

Minimum 44px on anything tappable; 46–54px in practice. A visible focus ring on every control — 2px BLUE_MID, offset 2px. Never outline: none.

Motion

Almost none. No scroll-triggered reveals — everything is readable the instant the page paints. Hover and focus transitions only, 120–180ms, and all of it disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.

The constraint that shapes everything above

The product claim is that we build sites which load before people give up. So this design spends nothing on a video hero, a carousel, a web font beyond the one brand face, or a framework. Every visual effect here is a CSS gradient or an inline SVG. If the finished site cannot score 7/7 on our own seven-point test, on mobile data, none of the rest of it matters.
