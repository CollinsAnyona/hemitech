Two minutes, your phone

Score your own website.

Seven checks, on mobile data: the way your clients actually open it. No developer needed.

  1. Loads in under 3 seconds on mobile data
  2. Your phone number dials when you tap it
  3. No pinching, no sideways scrolling
  4. Line one says what you do, before scrolling
  5. One obvious next step, not five
  6. The padlock is there, with no “Not secure” warning
  7. Something on it was updated this year
6–7Your site works. Let’s talk about making it convert.
4–5You’re leaking enquiries quietly. Usually three fixes, not a rebuild.
0–3It’s costing you clients today.

Most sites scoring 4 or 5 don’t need rebuilding. We’ll tell you if yours doesn’t.
