The free audit

Score your own website in two minutes.

Seven checks, on your phone, on mobile data: the way your clients actually open it. No developer needed, and no obligation to buy anything.

Most sites scoring 4 or 5 don’t need rebuilding. We’ll tell you if yours doesn’t.

Run the seven checks

The seven checks

  • Loads in under 3 seconds on mobile data
  • Your phone number dials when you tap it
  • No pinching. No sideways scrolling.
  • Line one says what you do, before scrolling
  • One obvious next step, not five
  • The padlock is there. No "Not secure".
  • Something on it was updated this year
