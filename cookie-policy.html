Cookie Policy

How cookies are used on this site, and how to control them.

Last updated: September 2026

What cookies we use

This site uses cookies set by Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it — which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors are located, and what device/browser they're using. We don't use cookies for advertising, and we don't run any advertising trackers on this site.

Why

Analytics cookies help us understand which pages are useful and where visitors run into friction, so we can improve the site over time.

Controlling cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Doing so won't affect your ability to browse this site or submit the contact form — it only limits our analytics data.

You can also opt out of Google Analytics specifically using Google's browser add-on.

Third-party cookies

Our contact form is processed by Formspree, and our social links lead to LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook — each of those third-party services may set their own cookies once you're on their site, governed by their own cookie policies, not this one.
