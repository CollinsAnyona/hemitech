Who this is for

The site works. Nobody is watching it. The certificate expires on a Saturday, the backup has not been tested, and the first you hear of any of it is a client asking why the site says "Not secure".

A site with no owner

Built by someone who has moved on, hosted on an account nobody can log into.

An organisation with a compliance duty

Backups, patching and access control that have to be demonstrable, not assumed.

A team with no technical staff

One number to call, and a response time in writing.

A system that matters on Monday

Monitoring that tells us it is down before your users do.

What you get

Managed hosting

Provisioned, configured and documented, in your own account where you want it.

Daily backups, tested

    One person who can decide. Not a committee. Someone who can say yes on a Tuesday.

  2. 02

    Access to the people who do the work. A few hours, early. It prevents a system nobody uses.

  3. 03

    Your existing data, as it is. Messy is fine. We would rather see the real spreadsheet than a cleaned-up one.
