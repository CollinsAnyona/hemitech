Privacy Policy

What we collect through this site, and how it's used.

Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

This policy covers hemitech.co.ke, operated by HemiTech, based in Nairobi, Kenya. For any privacy questions, contact us at hello@hemitech.co.ke.

What we collect

We collect personal data in two ways:

  • Contact form submissions: when you fill out the form on our Contact page, we collect your name, email address, service interest, and message. This is submitted directly to Formspree, our form-processing provider, and forwarded to us by email.
  • Site analytics: we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site (pages viewed, general location, device type). Google Analytics uses cookies to do this — see our Cookie Policy for details.

How we use it

Contact form data is used solely to respond to your enquiry. Analytics data is used in aggregate to understand and improve the site — we don't use it to identify individual visitors. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

Who else sees it

Two processors handle data on our behalf: Formspree (contact form submissions) and Google Analytics (site usage data). Both are bound by their own privacy and data-processing terms, which you can review on their respective websites.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by us, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@hemitech.co.ke and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

Data retention

Contact form enquiries are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and maintain a reasonable business record, after which they're deleted. Analytics data is retained per Google Analytics' standard retention settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site or our tools change. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.
